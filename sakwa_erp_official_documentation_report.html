1. Executive Summary & Corporate Profile

Sakwa Canneries & Exports (Pvt) Ltd is a premier Sri Lankan seafood canning and export facility situated in Baddegama, Southern Province. The company specializes in processing high-quality canned fish products, including 425g Canned Mackerel in Tomato Sauce, Canned Mackerel in Brine, and Canned Tuna in Oil for domestic and international markets.

To ensure 100% compliance with international HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point), SLSI, and EU Export Quality Standards, this custom Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Quality Control (QC) System was engineered specifically for Sakwa Canneries' operational workflow.

  • Frontend Core: Semantic HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript ES6+ (Zero framework overhead for sub-second execution speeds).

3. Comprehensive Breakdown of Core ERP Modules (10 Modules)

3.1 ❄️ Real-Time Cold Room IoT Temperature Monitoring

Connected to Dixell XR06CX Digital Controller simulating live -18.4 °C micro-fluctuations, setpoint monitoring (-18.0 °C), and live compressor status badges.

3.2 🐟 Fish Arrival Note (FAN) Module

Automated batch code generator (FISH-YYYY-MM-DD-01), gross/tare/net weight math engine, and automatic inward updating to Raw Material Stock.

3.3 📜 Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Module

Multi-item inward logging with automatic line total (Qty × Price) and grand total math, automatically populating stock inventory tables.

3.4 📦 Stock Issuing to Production Lines

Issues raw fish, salt, oil, and empty cans from warehouse storage to canning production lines while creating a Work-In-Progress (WIP) production batch.

3.5 📊 Master Stock Inventory & Export Suite

Manages Raw Material Stock, Packaging Stock, WIP Production Stock, and Finished Goods Stock with 1-click Excel (.xlsx) Export and PDF Printing.

3.6 🔬 HACCP Double Seam Quality Control (QC) Engine

4-Point measurement matrix (SL, BH, CH), real-time overlap % math engine, automated PASS ✅ / FAIL ❌ badge, and printable audit report matching factory Seam.xlsx layout.

3.7 ⚙️ Master Data & Dynamic Dropdown Manager

Centralized control panel to add, edit, or remove Fish Species, Box Types, Suppliers, Packaging, Departments, and Staff options across all dropdowns.

3.8 ✏️ Universal Record Editing & Deleting Engine

Edit (✏️) and Delete (🗑️) buttons across all stock and QC history tables with instant local storage recalculations.

3.9 👥 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Dedicated profiles for Storekeeper, Quality Control Officer, and Plant Director.

3.10 📱 Dark Mode & 100% Mobile Responsiveness Suite

Off-canvas mobile drawer navigation, mobile header logout button, and touch-optimized horizontal scrolling tables.
