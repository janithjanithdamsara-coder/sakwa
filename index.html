About Sakwa Canneries

Leading Fish Canning & Processing Plant in Baddegama

Sakwa Canneries & Exports (Pvt) Ltd operates a modern seafood canning facility located at Baddegama, Sri Lanka. Our state-of-the-art retort sterilization, blast freezing, and packaging lines ensure every can meets international quality standards.

  • Strict adherence to HACCP & SLS quality protocols
  • Complete raw material traceability from vessel arrival to finished cans
  • Supporting local coastal fishing communities
